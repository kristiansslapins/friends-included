Transaction records

New transaction

Recording and approval are separate. Your selected role identifies the submitter automatically.

Link a Telegram account

First send /start to the bot in a private chat. Enter the numeric user ID returned by the bot. Relinking an account never changes the submitter or destination of an existing bot transaction.

From submission to decision

  1. Choose an employee. Salespeople enter sales and proposed commission splits. Kevin enters expenses. Each employee sees their own submissions.
  2. Choose Svetlana to approve. Open a pending record, check its proposal, and confirm or change the split or allocation.
  3. Check delivery. Records display Sheets synchronization and Telegram notification status. Retry incomplete deliveries on the same record.
  4. Review the company result. Pending sales are excluded. All saved expenses are included immediately. Commissions are earned on approved sales only.
